Los Angeles ground station

Six windows, one continuous scene. Flight and Drive simulate travel over real Los Angeles streets. Map shows the same position from above.

Keyboard: F flight · D drive · M map · Space hold · A auto/manual · ← → steer · W/S speed · ↑ ↓ altitude in flight · R reset. The nine displays on each side show available functions. The bottom circles are reserved for physical controllers.

Drive demo follows a saved street route. Manual driving is free movement, without collision or traffic simulation. Amber buildings use OSM footprints with recorded heights where available; other heights are estimates. The local model uses a flat ground surface.
